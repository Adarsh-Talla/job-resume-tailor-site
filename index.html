A resume-tailoring assistant for job seekers

Make every application more relevant.

Job Resume Tailor helps you compare your resume with a job description, identify relevant keywords and gaps, and create a clear, ATS-friendly resume draft.

What it can help with

  • Assess how your experience aligns with a role.
  • Identify important skills and keywords in the job description.
  • Highlight missing or unsupported requirements honestly.
  • Rewrite resume content without inventing qualifications or experience.
  • Generate a DOCX resume and an estimated ATS keyword-coverage score.

Important: ATS scores are estimates for guidance, not official results from an employer's applicant tracking system. Always review the final resume for accuracy.

Use Job Resume Tailor inside ChatGPT. Upload your resume and the job description when prompted.
