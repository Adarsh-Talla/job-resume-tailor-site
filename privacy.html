Privacy Policy

This policy describes the data practices of the Job Resume Tailor plugin as provided by its developer, Adarsh Talla.

Information you provide

You may provide a resume, job description, and related instructions when using the plugin in ChatGPT. These materials can contain personal information such as your name, contact details, education and employment history.

Developer-operated storage and sharing

The current plugin package does not operate a separate developer-hosted server, maintain an independent resume database, or intentionally send resume content to an external processing service. The developer does not independently collect or sell the resume files through the plugin.

ChatGPT platform processing

Use of the plugin takes place within ChatGPT. Information you submit is handled by the ChatGPT service and is subject to OpenAI's applicable privacy policy, product settings and data controls. This developer policy does not describe or override OpenAI's platform-level processing, retention or security practices. Please review OpenAI's privacy information and your ChatGPT data controls before uploading sensitive documents.

Data minimization

Only provide information needed for the resume task. You may remove details such as your full address or personal contact information before uploading a resume if they are not needed for the task.

Children

This tool is intended for people seeking employment and is not designed for children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the plugin's functionality or data practices change. For questions, use the support page. If the plugin is changed to store or share user data independently, this policy must be updated before that change is made available.
